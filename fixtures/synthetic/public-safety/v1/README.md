# Synthetic public-safety receipt

This newly authored fixture demonstrates the machine-readable exact-candidate
receipt and its deterministic human rendering. The hexadecimal identities are
synthetic and do not refer to a repository, machine, owner, or release.

The fixture proves schema validation and rendering only. It is not visibility
approval evidence. A final candidate receipt remains external because writing
it into the audited clone would invalidate its clean-worktree and exact-content
claims.
