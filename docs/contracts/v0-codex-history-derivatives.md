# v0 authorized Codex-history derivatives

The reference path accepts one explicitly selected local task only. It admits
an allowlisted title, summary, decisions, and open questions into a
vendor-neutral derivative record with source-task and transformation
provenance. Raw transcripts, attachments, credentials, browser state,
absolute paths, and unrelated task material are excluded; raw archives are
not canonical records and require a separate encrypted recovery policy.

The declassification receipt records admitted fields, excluded categories,
uncertainty, and retention behavior. The fixture is synthetic and ends at
curated records and a receipt; it creates no task, route, authority, or
execution request.
